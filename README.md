<a href="https://music-town.straw.page">Put yourself in our music  list! <i>(max 2 songs for 1 person unless complimenting/friend groups)</i></a>
<br>
<br>
You can also nominate songs for you and your partner/s, just pick a song that reminds you of you and them <br>
This can also work with friends and/or friend groups! <br>
Example: "I would like to nominate [friend name] and [my name] to be [song name] by [artist]." <br>
Or <br>
"I would like to nominate [friend group name] to be [song name] by [artist]" <br>
<br>
You can also compliment people and say certain songs remind you of them!
<br>
Example: "To [github user], [song name] by [artist] reminds me a lot of you, from [gitub user #2]/anonymous"
<br>
<br>
Any type of music is allowed, including OSTs, USTs, etc.! (Unless problematic music artists)
